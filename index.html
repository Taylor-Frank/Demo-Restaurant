Our Story

More Than Just a Grill

Lucky 7 Grill is a family-owned spot right in the heart of Cicero, IL. We built this place on the belief that good food doesn't need to be complicated — just fresh, fire-broiled, and made with care.

From the moment we opened our doors, the community welcomed us with open arms. Whether you're stopping in for a quick breakfast at 6am or settling in for ribs on a Friday night, you're always welcome here.
