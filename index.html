More Than Just a Grill

Lucky 7 Grill is a family-owned restaurant in the heart of Cicero, IL. We built this place on the belief that good food doesn't need to be complicated — just fresh, fire-broiled, and made with care.

From early morning breakfast to late night dinners, we're here for Cicero seven days a week. Whether you're grabbing a quick bite or feeding the whole crew, you're always welcome here.
